## The Brief

A concept campaign demonstrating how one strong key visual can stretch across posters, digital banners and packaging accents while staying unmistakably itself.

## Concept

The idea: product as protagonist. Every composition gives the object stage-like space, supported by oversized typography that behaves like scenery.

## Art Direction

A warm gradient world keeps the mood premium; the accent colour appears only on the call to action so attention lands in one place. Grids are strict, crops are bold.

## Outcome

The system flexes from A0 poster to 300-pixel banner without losing hierarchy — proof of a campaign built on structure, not decoration.
