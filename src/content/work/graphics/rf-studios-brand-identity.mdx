## The Brief

Design a self-initiated identity for a multidisciplinary studio whose work spans code, photo, design and film — without letting any single discipline dominate the mark.

## Process

**Research** began with positioning: the brand needed to read as engineering-grade and human at once. **Concept** work reduced dozens of sketches to a monogram built from two confident letterforms. During **art direction**, a near-black canvas, electric blue accent and Manrope typeface were locked in. The **design** phase produced the full lock-up suite, spacing rules and misuse examples. **Final delivery** packaged everything for print and screen.

## Result

A flexible identity system where the wordmark whispers and the work shouts — exactly right for a portfolio-first studio. This website itself follows the system.
