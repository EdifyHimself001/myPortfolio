## The Brief

A template system concept that lets a small team publish consistent, on-brand social content in minutes instead of hours.

## Concept

Consistency comes from constraints: fixed type hierarchy, one photo treatment and a repeating corner device that makes every post recognisable at thumbnail size.

## Design

The kit covers announcements, quotes, statistics and event reminders. Each layout is tested at feed size before detail is added — if it fails small, it fails.

## Delivery

Templates ship as organised component files with usage notes, so non-designers can swap copy and imagery without breaking the system.
