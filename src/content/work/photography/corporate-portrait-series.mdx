## About This Series

A studio portrait series exploring how much character a single light, one backdrop and five minutes of conversation can hold.

## Approach

The setup stays deliberately simple: one key light shaped by a large modifier, subtle fill and a neutral seamless backdrop. Direction is conversational rather than posed — the goal is a person, not a template.

## Consistency

Because the lighting recipe repeats across sitters, the series reads as one voice — useful for teams that need portraits which sit well together on an About page.
