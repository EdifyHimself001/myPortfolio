## About This Series

Event coverage shot as reportage rather than a checklist — arrivals, speeches, reactions and the texture of the room between programme items.

## Approach

I work quietly at the edge of the room, anticipating moments instead of staging them. Wide frames establish place; tight frames find faces. The result tells the story of the day in sequence.

## Delivery

Images are edited into a narrative order suitable for press recaps, annual reports and social storytelling.
