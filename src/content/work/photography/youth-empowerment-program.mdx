## About This Series

A documentary-style photo essay following the everyday energy of a youth skills programme — workshops, mentorship moments and the quiet focus between them.

## Approach

I shot available light wherever possible, working slowly and returning over multiple days so people forgot the camera. Portraits are made in the spaces where participants already spend time, not against backdrops.

## Selection

The edit favours gestures over poses: hands learning a tool, laughter mid-sentence, the pause before an answer. Colour grading stays close to natural skin tones with gentle contrast.
