## The Idea

A short brand film built on a simple spine — a person, a craft, a turning point. No voiceover until the final act; the visuals carry the first two minutes.

## Approach

The edit is cut to rhythm rather than to script. Shots are chosen for motion continuity so the film breathes instead of flashing. Colour stays warm and honest, with the accent blue reserved for the closing card.

## Craft Notes

Sound design does quiet work here: room tone, fabric, footsteps. The mix leaves headroom so the single line of narration lands with weight.
