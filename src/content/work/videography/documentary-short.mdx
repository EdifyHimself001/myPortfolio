## The Subject

A portrait of a craftsperson told in their own words — patience as a skill, repetition as mastery.

## Approach

Interviews were recorded first, then filmed around them. B-roll answers the interview: when hands are described, we see hands. Nothing is staged; the documentary trusts real time.

## Form

Long takes with slow pushes keep attention on process rather than cuts. The grade leans into available light, preserving the honesty of the location.
