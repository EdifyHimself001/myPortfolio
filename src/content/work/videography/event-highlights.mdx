## The Goal

Turn a full day of event footage into a ninety-second reel that feels like being in the room — energy up front, meaning at the end.

## Approach

Structure beats spectacle: establishing shots set place, speaker moments carry authority, crowd reactions provide pulse. The music track is selected to match the natural pacing of the day, not to fight it.

## Delivery

Cut-downs are planned from the start: the hero reel plus square and vertical edits sized for social platforms.
