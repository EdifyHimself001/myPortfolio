## Overview

This concept project explores what a modern pan-African payment dashboard could feel like: multi-currency balances, mobile money and card flows unified behind one honest, legible interface.

## The Problem

Merchants working across several payment rails reconcile figures by hand. Interfaces show raw transaction logs instead of answers, and trust suffers when numbers are hard to verify.

## The Approach

The design started from the merchant's core question — *"did the money arrive?"* — and worked backwards. Every screen states balances in the currency the merchant thinks in, with conversion details one level deeper.

## The Solution

The dashboard pairs a settlement timeline with anomaly highlights. A restrained type system gives numerals generous spacing, and status colours are chosen to survive both bright sunlight and colour-blind vision.

> Portfolio demonstration project — no real financial data or partners involved.
