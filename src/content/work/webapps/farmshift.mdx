## Overview

FarmShift is a concept web application exploring how small agricultural teams plan a week of work without paperwork. It was designed mobile-first, because the field — not the office — is where the decisions happen.

## The Problem

Small farm operations often coordinate people through group chats and whiteboards. When plans change at sunrise, nobody is sure who is doing what, and harvest windows get missed.

## The Approach

I interviewed the rhythm of farm work itself: short bursts of attention, gloved hands, unreliable connectivity. That led to a large-target interface, offline-tolerant data caching and a week view that fits one screen.

## The Solution

A shared board shows each day as a column of colour-coded task cards. Dragging a card reassigns it; everyone's phone updates when they regain signal. The visual language borrows from weather apps — glanceable, calm, high contrast in daylight.
